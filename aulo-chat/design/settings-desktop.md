## Layout

A window over the dimmed main window (scrim), 860 × 680:

- **macOS:** traffic lights, a sidebar of tabs (`aulo-chat/Settings tab`) under "Settings",
  and the content with the tab title.
- **Windows 11:** a title bar ("aulo settings", Close), a navigation pane of the kit's
  Navigation view items with icons, and the content with the tab title; each row is its own
  card.
- **Linux:** a header bar whose centred title is the tab name, Close at the right, a
  navigation list of Nav rows, and the content grouped in cards with hairlines.

Rows are `aulo-chat/Settings row`: a title, an optional detail line, and one control at the
right (value, status dot, switch, picker or button). A footnote under the groups states the
rule that applies to the tab.

Platform copy differs where the OS differs: the key store name, home paths (`~/` or `~\`),
Trash or Recycle Bin, device names, and "this Mac" / "this PC" / "this computer".

## Behaviour

- Secrets are entered once and kept in the OS key store; every screen shows only the handle.
- "Click only" rules cannot be satisfied by voice (see `approval.md`).
- A grant made from an Approval card appears under Grants and can be revoked there.
- An extension that fails is marked Skipped, never blocks the app.
